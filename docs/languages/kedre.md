# Kedre

Kedre ("the Tongue") is the language of the **Vairean Empire**, spoken in the imperial city of Keras and the old Kingdom of Reinovaire, in the north-west of Valia. It arrived in Thiolden with Rechtar Vaire's conquest (37–51 a.v.e.) and his daughter Doir Vaire's rule over the Vilnayan towns. It left a lasting layer of words in Norsund, much as Norman French did in English.

Kedre is a **distant cousin** of Norsund. Both descend from Proto-Valian, so many basic words correspond: Norsund *thiol* and Kedre *telo* both mean brave, and the Norsund goddess **Lysmar** is the Kedre goddess **Luxis**.

The source files are in `records/languages/kedre/`:

- `grammar.yaml`: the grammar sketch
- `dictionary.yaml`: about 150 words, including the sources of every Kedre loan in Norsund
- `texts.yaml`: the decree of year zero and the blessing of the Vaire bloodline

## How it looks and sounds

Kedre looks Latin to a reader on purpose: it is the conquerors' polished, imperial voice.

- Most words end in a vowel: *kaimo* "home", *legia* "legion", *luxi* "light".
- Stress falls on the second-to-last syllable: KE-ras, ke-RI-a, rei-no-VAI-re.
- It has no *th* and no *y*. Where Norsund has *th* or *f* from the old breathy consonants, Kedre often has *t* or *f*: *telo* beside *thiol*, *fovo* beside *dov*.
- *k* is always written k (Keras); *x* means ks (Luxis); the old spelling *cht* in Rechtar is a rasping [xt].

## Grammar at a glance

**No gender, no case, no articles.** Plurals change the final vowel: *posto* "heir", *posti* "heirs".

**Head first.** The main word comes first and its owner or description follows, the opposite of Norsund:

| Kedre | Word for word | Meaning | Norsund puts it the other way round |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reinovaire | realm Vaire | the realm of Vaire | — |
| Domo Luxis | house Luxis | the Church of Light | Lysmari heim "Lysmar's house" |
| luxi tue | light your | your light | dys lys "your light" |

**The dignity suffix -s.** It marks gods, imperial titles and holy cities: *luxi* "light" → **Luxis** the goddess; *kera* "crown" → **Keras** "the Crowned" city. *Keria*, "crown-land", is the Empire itself.

**Verbs.** Two classes, *-are* and *-ire*, with person endings: *luxo, luxi, luxa, luxamo, luxate, luxan* ("I bless ... they bless"). The past adds *-av-* (*luxava* "blessed"). Participles in *-ato* often serve as nouns: *jubato* "decree", literally "the commanded".

**Decrees put the verb first.** Ordinary speech is subject-verb-object, but a proclamation begins with the verb: *Juba Rechtar Vaire ...* "Commands Rechtar Vaire ...".

**One subordinator.** *ke* introduces every "that" and "who" clause. Norsund borrowed it.

## Sample: the decree of year zero

> Juba Rechtar Vaire, keraro ed primaro:
> Oie nasa Keria, ed Reinovaire kresa in Keria.
> Luxis luxava sangia Vaire.
> Kai ke sta kontra keraro, sta kontra Luxis.
> Aro nulo, Keras.

*Rechtar Vaire, emperor and first priest, commands:
Today the Empire is born, and the realm of Vaire grows into the Empire.
Luxis has blessed the bloodline of Vaire.
Whoever stands against the emperor stands against Luxis.
Year zero, at Keras.*

## Kedre words in Norsund

Kedre words entered Norsund in layers.

| Layer | When | What happened | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Occupation | 37–51 a.v.e. and Doir Vaire's rule | final vowel dropped, double consonants simplified, stress moved to the front | *murra* → **mur** "wall", *merkadu* → **merk** "market", *kampo* → **kamp** "square", *porta* → **port** "gate", *turra* → **tur** "tower", *rivia* → **rie** "canal", *ke* → **ke** "that" |
| Learned | after 100 a.v.e., from books | kept whole, Kedre stress kept | **alkimia**, **medikina**, **kupola** |

Some words were borrowed twice. Norsund has both *tur* "tower" (everyday) and *turra* "fortress-tower" (in military treatises).

The traffic went both ways: Kedre borrowed **runa** from Norsund to name the rune magic of Thiolden, while its own *runo* means only "secret".

## Imperial names explained

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Keras | the Crowned (city) |
| Keria | the crown-land, the Empire |
| Reinovaire | realm of Vaire |
| Vaire | the imperial house (*-aire* "house, domain") |
| Rechtar | ruler (*rektare* "to rule") |
| Luxis | Light, the goddess (Norsund Lysmar) |
| Voidet | "of the emptiness", the family name of Rundir Voidet |

## Renamed places

Two place names in the lore were real English towns. They were replaced with these Kedre names in the lore records on 2026-11-19:

- **Exeter → Exaire**, "domain of the springs"
- **Arbury → Arbaire**, "domain of the trees"

## For writers

- Use Kedre for imperial voices: decrees, priests of the Church of Light, Vairean officers, alchemists.
- In Thiolden, Kedre words in Norsund mouths should be the worn-down loans (*mur*, *port*, *merk*), not full Kedre.
- Keep the head-first order and the verb-first decree style. Those two habits make Kedre sound unlike Norsund at a glance.
