    Translate

    English to Norsund uses a small rule-based grammar: simple clauses, tenses, modals, negation, questions, possessives and evidential hints. Norsund to English gives a word-by-word Leipzig gloss and a rough reading. Always check the gloss before using a line in canon.

    Write

    Lysrit is Auristar's sacred and civic script: consonants are strokes on the seven-point grid of the Auristar emblem and vowels are stars. Runes are Thiolden's everyday script. Ordinary writing never glows; a glowing rune is charged with aether, which is rune magic and forbidden in Auristar.

    Charged runes are rune magic. In Auristar they are forbidden; use this only for magic scenes outside the city.

    Names

    Place names are modifiers plus one generic head (Nor + skyl = Norskyl, "North Falls"). The people of a place take the first element plus -nay. Person names are a given name and a parent-name in -ung, "child of".

    Numbers

    Norsund counts in tens. Five is thamm, "hand", and ten is tamm, worn down from "two hands". In Auristar seven is stev, reshaped by star. Old markets count in the long hundred (langhaun) of 120.
